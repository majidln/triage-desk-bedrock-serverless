# Privacy and your data

## Where data lives

Flowboard data is hosted in the **EU (Frankfurt)**. It isn't possible to choose another region.

## Protection

Data is **encrypted in transit and at rest**.

## Data processing agreement

Business customers can request a data processing agreement (DPA) through support.

## Deleting a workspace

Only the **workspace owner** can delete a workspace, under *Settings → General → Delete workspace*. Support can restore it within 30 days. See *Deleted items, trash and backups*.

If you cancel your subscription instead, your data stays read-only for 90 days. See *Refunds and cancellations*.

## Getting your data out

See *Exporting your data*.
