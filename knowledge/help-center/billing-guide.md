# Billing guide

Plans, prices, refunds and payments.

## Plans and pricing

### The plans

| Plan | Price | Users | Includes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Free** | $0 | Up to 5 | 3 boards, 100 MB storage, community support only |
| **Team** | $8 per user/month billed annually, or $10 billed monthly | Unlimited | Unlimited boards, 10 GB storage per user, integrations, email support |
| **Business** | $16 per user/month billed annually, or $20 billed monthly | Unlimited | Everything in Team, plus SSO (SAML), audit log, guest permissions, a 99.9% uptime SLA and priority support |

Prices are in US dollars and don't include tax.

### How seats are billed

- You pay for each **active member** of your workspace.
- **Guests** are free on Business. Team doesn't have guests.
- **Adding seats** during a billing period is charged right away, pro-rata for the rest of the period.
- **Removing seats** takes effect at your next renewal. Removed seats are not partially refunded, so an invoice can show more seats than you have members today.

### Changing plans

- **Upgrades** take effect immediately.
- **Downgrades** take effect at the end of your current billing period.

### Discounts

Registered **non-profits** and **education** organizations get **50% off**. There are no other discounts, and support can't offer custom ones.

## Refunds and cancellations

### The 14-day refund window

A new subscription can be refunded in full within **14 days of its first payment**. This applies to monthly and annual plans.

### After 14 days

| Plan | What happens if you cancel |
|---|---|
| **Monthly** | No refund |
| **Annual** | Refunded **pro-rata for the remaining full months** |

### Duplicate or incorrect charges

If you were charged twice, or charged when you shouldn't have been, for example after cancelling, the charge is **always refunded in full**, whenever it happened.

### How refunds are handled

Refunds are handled by our **billing team**. Support confirms that your request has been received and passes it on. Once a refund is issued, it reaches your card in **5–10 business days**. We can't give you an exact date.

### Cancelling

Only the **billing owner** can cancel a subscription. See *Payments and invoices* below.

After you cancel, your workspace's data stays available **read-only for 90 days**, then it's deleted. Export anything you need before then; see *Exporting your data* in the *Data and reliability* guide.

## Payments and invoices

### The billing owner

Each workspace has one **billing owner**. Only the billing owner can:
- change the payment method
- see invoices
- cancel the subscription

If your billing owner has left, a **workspace admin** can transfer billing ownership under *Settings → Billing → Transfer ownership*.

### Updating your card

Enter payment details only on the **billing page**. Flowboard never asks for card numbers by email or in a support ticket. If you've sent card details in a ticket or email, contact your bank about the card.

### Payment methods

Flowboard accepts **credit and debit cards** (Visa, Mastercard, American Express). We don't accept PayPal, bank transfer or invoice payment.

### Tax details and billing cycle

- The billing owner can add a **tax or VAT ID** on the billing page. It applies to future invoices, not past ones.
- Switching between monthly and annual billing takes effect at your **next renewal**.

### When a payment fails

1. Flowboard retries the payment for **7 days**.
2. After that, the workspace becomes **read-only** until a payment succeeds.

Nothing is deleted while the workspace is read-only. Once the billing owner updates the payment method and the payment goes through, the workspace works normally again.
