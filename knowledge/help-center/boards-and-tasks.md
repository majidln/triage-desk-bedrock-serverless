# Boards and tasks

## Tasks

- A task can have a **description**, an **assignee**, a **due date**, **subtasks**, **comments** and **attachments**.
- **@mention** a teammate in a comment to notify them. See *Notifications*.

## Custom fields

Custom fields add your own information to tasks. A field can be one of five types: **text**, **number**, **date**, **dropdown** or **checkbox**.

## Timeline view

The timeline shows your tasks along a calendar. A task appears on it when it has a start date and a due date. The timeline is not a Gantt chart; see *Feature requests*.

## Archive or delete

| | Archive | Delete |
|---|---|---|
| Visible to the team | Hidden | Gone |
| Can be restored | Any time | For 30 days, from the Trash |

See *Deleted items, trash and backups*.
