# Getting help

What to try first, and how to reach support.

## Troubleshooting

Before you contact support, a few steps resolve many problems.

1. **Check the status page** at `status.flowboard.example`. If there's an incident, updates are posted there.
2. **Reload** the page, or restart the app.
3. **Try another browser or device.** The web app, iOS and Android all show the same data.
4. **Find out who is affected:** just you, a few people, or the whole workspace. This helps us a lot.
5. **Note the exact error message**, and what you did just before it.

### Then contact support

See *Contacting support* below for channels and response times. Never include a password, a 2FA code or card details in a message.

## Contacting support

### Channels and response times

| Plan | Where to ask | First response target |
|---|---|---|
| Free | Community forum | None |
| Team | Email or in-app | 1 business day |
| Business | Email or in-app, priority queue | 4 business hours; **critical issues: 1 hour, 24/7** |

**Business hours** are Monday to Friday, 08:00–18:00 CET.

### Language

Support is available in **English only**.

### What helps us help you

- What you were doing, and what happened instead
- Any error message, word for word
- Whether it affects one person or your whole team

Never include a password, a 2FA code or card details. See *Account security* in the *Accounts and security* guide.
