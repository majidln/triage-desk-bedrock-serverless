# Contacting support

## Channels and response times

| Plan | Where to ask | First response target |
|---|---|---|
| Free | Community forum | None |
| Team | Email or in-app | 1 business day |
| Business | Email or in-app, priority queue | 4 business hours; **critical issues: 1 hour, 24/7** |

**Business hours** are Monday to Friday, 08:00–18:00 CET.

## Language

Support is available in **English only**.

## What helps us help you

- What you were doing, and what happened instead
- Any error message, word for word
- Whether it affects one person or your whole team

Never include a password, a 2FA code or card details. See *Account security*.
