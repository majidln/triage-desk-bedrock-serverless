# Business plan features

These are available on the **Business** plan only. See *Plans and pricing*.

## Single sign-on (SAML)

SSO works with any **SAML 2.0** identity provider. A workspace admin sets it up under *Settings → Security*. When SSO is enforced, password login is turned off for everyone except the workspace owner. See *Account security*.

## Audit log

The audit log records **sign-ins, membership changes and settings changes**. Admins can view it under *Settings → Audit log*. Entries are kept for **12 months**.

## Guests

Invite clients and partners as free guests. See *Members, roles and guests*.

## Uptime SLA and priority support

See *Uptime, incidents and maintenance* and *Contacting support*.
