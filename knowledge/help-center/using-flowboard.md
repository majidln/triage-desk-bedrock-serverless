# Using Flowboard

Getting started, working with boards and tasks, files, notifications, apps, and features we don't have.

## Getting started with Flowboard

Flowboard is a project-management app for teams. You organize work on boards, and each board holds tasks.

### What you can do

- **Boards** group the work of a project or a team.
- **Tasks** live on a board. A task can have **subtasks**, **comments** and **file attachments**.
- **Custom fields** add your own information to tasks.
- The **timeline view** shows tasks along their dates.

### Words you'll see

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Workspace | Your organization's space in Flowboard. Boards, members and billing belong to a workspace |
| Member | A person in your workspace. Members are billed as seats |
| Guest | Someone outside your organization invited to work with you. Business plan only |
| Workspace admin | A member who manages the workspace's settings and members |
| Billing owner | The one person who manages payment, invoices and cancellation |

## Boards and tasks

### Tasks

- A task can have a **description**, an **assignee**, a **due date**, **subtasks**, **comments** and **attachments**.
- **@mention** a teammate in a comment to notify them. See *Notifications* below.

### Custom fields

Custom fields add your own information to tasks. A field can be one of five types: **text**, **number**, **date**, **dropdown** or **checkbox**.

### Timeline view

The timeline shows your tasks along a calendar. A task appears on it when it has a start date and a due date. The timeline is not a Gantt chart; see *Feature requests* below.

### Archive or delete

| | Archive | Delete |
|---|---|---|
| Visible to the team | Hidden | Gone |
| Can be restored | Any time | For 30 days, from the Trash |

See *Deleted items, trash and backups* in the *Data and reliability* guide.

## Attachments and storage

### File size

| Plan | Largest file |
|---|---|
| Free | 25 MB |
| Team and Business | 250 MB |

### Storage

Each plan includes an amount of storage; see *Plans and pricing* in the *Billing guide*. Storage is **shared by the whole workspace**.

When the workspace is full, new uploads are blocked until you delete files or upgrade your plan. Existing files stay available.

### If an upload fails

- Check the file is under the size limit for your plan.
- Check whether the workspace has run out of storage.
- If neither applies and uploads fail for several people, tell support.

## Notifications

Flowboard can notify you in three ways:

| Channel | Where |
|---|---|
| In-app | The notification bell |
| Email | Your account's email address |
| Push | The iOS and Android apps |

### Settings

Choose what you get on each channel under *Settings → Notifications*. **Mentions** and **assignments** are on by default. You can switch to a **daily digest** instead of individual emails.

Settings are per person: each member sets their own.

### Not receiving notifications?

- Check your settings for that channel.
- For email, check your spam folder.
- For push, check that notifications are allowed for Flowboard in your phone's settings.
- If nobody on your team receives any, tell support.

## Apps and platforms

### Where Flowboard runs

- **Web:** `app.flowboard.example`
- **iOS** app
- **Android** app

Sign in to the apps with the same account as on the web. Your boards and tasks are the same on every platform, and the apps send push notifications (see *Notifications* above). If one app has a problem, you can keep working in another, for example on the web.

### What isn't available

- **No desktop app.** On a computer, use Flowboard in your browser.
- **No offline mode.** Flowboard needs an internet connection on every platform.

Requests for either are logged as feature requests. See *Feature requests* below.

## Feature requests

### Features Flowboard doesn't have

| Feature | Closest thing today |
|---|---|
| Gantt chart | The **timeline view** |
| Dark mode | None |
| Time tracking | None |
| Offline mode or a desktop app | The web app in your browser; see *Apps and platforms* above |
| On-premise or self-hosted version | None. Flowboard is hosted only |

### How we handle requests

Every feature request is logged, and we thank you for it. We can't promise that a feature will be built, or say when it might be released.
