# Troubleshooting

Before you contact support, a few steps resolve many problems.

1. **Check the status page** at `status.flowboard.example`. If there's an incident, updates are posted there.
2. **Reload** the page, or restart the app.
3. **Try another browser or device.** The web app, iOS and Android all show the same data.
4. **Find out who is affected:** just you, a few people, or the whole workspace. This helps us a lot.
5. **Note the exact error message**, and what you did just before it.

## Then contact support

See *Contacting support* for channels and response times. Never include a password, a 2FA code or card details in a message.
