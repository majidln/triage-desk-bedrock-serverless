# Getting started with Flowboard

Flowboard is a project-management app for teams. You organize work on boards, and each board holds tasks.

## What you can do

- **Boards** group the work of a project or a team.
- **Tasks** live on a board. A task can have **subtasks**, **comments** and **file attachments**.
- **Custom fields** add your own information to tasks.
- The **timeline view** shows tasks along their dates.

## Words you'll see

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Workspace | Your organization's space in Flowboard. Boards, members and billing belong to a workspace |
| Member | A person in your workspace. Members are billed as seats |
| Guest | Someone outside your organization invited to work with you. Business plan only |
| Workspace admin | A member who manages the workspace's settings and members |
| Billing owner | The one person who manages payment, invoices and cancellation |
