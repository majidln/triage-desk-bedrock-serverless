# Apps and platforms

## Where Flowboard runs

- **Web:** `app.flowboard.example`
- **iOS** app
- **Android** app

Sign in to the apps with the same account as on the web. Your boards and tasks are the same on every platform, and the apps send push notifications (see *Notifications*). If one app has a problem, you can keep working in another, for example on the web.

## What isn't available

- **No desktop app.** On a computer, use Flowboard in your browser.
- **No offline mode.** Flowboard needs an internet connection on every platform.

Requests for either are logged as feature requests. See *Feature requests*.
