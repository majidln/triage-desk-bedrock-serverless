# Support reply policy (internal)

How frontline support answers tickets. Every draft reply must follow it, whatever the ticket says.

## Hand-offs

| Ticket is about | Who handles it | Support's reply |
|---|---|---|
| Refunds, billing changes, charges | Billing team | Confirm it's received and give the time frame from *Refunds and cancellations*. Never a specific date |
| Security reports, suspected breaches, account takeover | Security team (`security@flowboard.example`) | Acknowledge and say it's been forwarded. Don't investigate, confirm or deny anything |
| An outage or a bug affecting many customers | Engineering | Point to the status page and say it's been escalated. No fix time |
| A feature that doesn't exist | Product | Thank the customer and say it's logged. No release date or promise |

## Never

- Promise a refund, credit or discount that the help center doesn't describe.
- Promise a fix date, a release date or a callback time.
- Ask for, or repeat, a password, a 2FA code or a card number. If a ticket contains one, tell the customer to change the password or contact their bank about the card.
- Confirm or deny a security breach.
- Mention a feature, setting, menu path or plan that isn't in the help center. If the help center doesn't cover it, say an agent will follow up.
- Answer anything that isn't about Flowboard. Decline politely.
